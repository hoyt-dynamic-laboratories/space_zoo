# Human-AI Development

All Space Zoo code was generated by AI under human direction. Most code-generation work was performed with OpenAI's **GPT-5.6 Sol**, while OpenAI's **GPT-6 Astra** contributed to some architecture and design work. 
Sol 6 snuck in at times...I tried using Sol 6.1 but I didn't really get along with it. Sol 5.6 is solid, and I begrudgingly used Astra 6 becasue it is good, but its humor is dryer than... 
Architecture, behavior, and implementation decisions were developed collaboratively between human and AI, and every accepted change was human reviewed, tested, and explicitly approved before being treated as part of the project.

This project is independently developed and is not sponsored or endorsed by OpenAI.

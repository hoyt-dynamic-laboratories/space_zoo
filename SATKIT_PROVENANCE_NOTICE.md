# Space Zoo — SatKit Attribution

Space Zoo uses Pluto orbital coefficients from SatKit 0.24.1,
`src/lpephem/planets.rs`, in its planetary ephemeris generator.
The incorporated material consists of numerical coefficients, not SatKit implementation code.

- Source: https://docs.rs/satkit/0.24.1/src/satkit/lpephem/planets.rs.html
- Upstream software license: MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- MIT license: https://github.com/ssmichael1/satkit/blob/v0.24.1/LICENSE-MIT
- Planetary-position equations: https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html
